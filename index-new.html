Your first visit

What to expect

Wear whatever you want Undress to your comfort level. You’ll be properly draped at all times — only the area being worked on is ever exposed.
Tell Gracie what you need She’ll ask about your pain points and preferences before starting. Speak up anytime during the session if you want more or less pressure.
Arrive on time Sessions start as scheduled and can’t extend for late arrivals. Give yourself 5 minutes to settle in.
You’ll feel great after Most people feel deeply relaxed, with relief from chronic tension that can last for days. Drink plenty of water afterward.
